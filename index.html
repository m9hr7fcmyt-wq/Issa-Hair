About Me

I’m Issa — a loctician who loves creating clean, stylish, and long‑lasting looks. I specialize in retwists, starter locs, invisible locs, butterfly locs, and protective styles that match your personality and lifestyle.

My chair is where precision meets creativity. I’m all about crisp parts, smooth finishes, and styles that stay fresh. If you want someone who cares about the details, respects your time, and keeps your hair looking flawless, you’re in the right place.

Your loc journey is personal — and I’m here to make sure it’s beautiful.
